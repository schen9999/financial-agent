The key variables an investor should monitor are the pace of cash burn relative to available liquidity, the on-time execution of the Lucid Gravity and midsize platform launches, and any signs of improving production efficiency or gross margin trajectory. Conversely, further production delays, additional large-scale capital raises that dilute shareholders, or a deterioration in luxury EV demand broadly would deepen the already significant concerns about the company's long-term viability as a standalone enterprise.
