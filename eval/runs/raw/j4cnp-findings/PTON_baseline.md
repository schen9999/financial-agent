Investors should watch four key variables closely: the trend in subscriber retention and churn rates quarter over quarter; whether services-segment margins expand as a share of the overall revenue mix; management's ability to demonstrate operating expense discipline relative to revenue growth; and the quality of guidance provided in upcoming quarterly results.
