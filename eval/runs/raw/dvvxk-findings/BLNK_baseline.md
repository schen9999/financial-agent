## Business Overview
Blink Charging is a leading owner, operator, and provider of EV charging equipment and networked services across the U.S. and international markets. The company operates through three primary business models: Blink-owned turnkey, Blink-owned hybrid, and host-owned arrangements, each with different revenue-sharing structures and contract terms.

## Strategic Restructuring (BlinkForward Initiative)
In May 2025, Blink announced a major restructuring plan aimed at achieving profitability and operational efficiency:
- Reduced global workforce from 513 to approximately 320 employees
- Transitioned to contract manufacturing (completed January 2026), eliminating in-house manufacturing facilities
- Reduced operating and general administrative expenses
- Shifted focus to intellectual property and customer experience

## Financial Activity
- Completed a $20 million funding round via public markets in December 2025
- Acquired Zemetric Inc. in July 2025, adding software-driven fleet management, energy management services, and a lower-cost Level 2 charger lineup

## Network Growth
As of December 31, 2025, Blink Network included approximately 66,350 connected chargers, with approximately 8,250 owned by the company. The company also operates car-sharing programs through its subsidiary Envoy Mobility.

## Industry Trends
The EV charging sector experienced significant growth in 2025, with 18,041 new DC fast charging ports added in the U.S. (30% year-over-year growth), improved network reliability in the low 90% range, and stable pricing between $0.45-$0.53 per kilowatt-hour.
