RAG — SEC HIGHLIGHTS:
[From Pinecone cache] # Key Takeaways from Blink Charging's Latest SEC Filings

## Strategic Restructuring and Operational Efficiency
In May 2025, Blink announced the BlinkForward Initiative, a strategic restructuring plan designed to accelerate the path to profitability and enhance operational efficiency. This included reducing the global workforce from 513 to approximately 320 employees, cutting operating and administrative expenses, and transitioning to contract manufacturing (completed in January 2026).

## Financial Actions
Blink completed a $20 million funding round via public markets in December 2025 to support expansion efforts.

## Strategic Acquisition
In July 2025, Blink acquired Zemetric Inc., filling product gaps in software-driven fleet management, energy management services, and lower-cost Level 2 charger hardware. Zemetric's CEO became Blink's Chief Technology Officer.

## Network Growth and Composition
As of December 31, 2025, approximately 66,350 chargers were connected to the Blink Network, consisting of roughly 58,850 Level 2 commercial chargers and 1,920 DC Fast Charging (DCFC) commercial chargers, with approximately 8,250 chargers owned by Blink.

## Market Expansion Focus
Blink is prioritizing expansion of its DC Fast Charging network through deployment of high-speed chargers in strategic, high-utilization locations.

## Product Portfolio Enhancement
The company expanded its offerings to include the Shasta Level 2 charger (from the Zemetric acquisition), featuring ISO 15118 readiness and "Plug & Charge" functionality, alongside existing Series 7, 8, and 10 families and international products.
