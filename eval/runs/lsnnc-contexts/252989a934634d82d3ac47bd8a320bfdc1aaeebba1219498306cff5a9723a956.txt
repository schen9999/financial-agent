- The company had 474 million daily active users (DAUs) as of Q4 2025, but faces ongoing pressure to maintain and grow this metric

- Future growth will need to come from older users or developing markets, which presents greater difficulty and expense

## Revenue Concentration Risk
- Advertising accounts for approximately 87% of revenue (down from 96% in 2023), though it's expected to remain the dominant revenue source
- Most advertisers lack long-term commitments and spend relatively small portions of their overall advertising budgets with Snapchat
- Some customers represent meaningful budget contributions, creating concentration risk

## Competitive and Operational Pressures
- Low barriers to entry and switching costs make user retention vulnerable
- Competitors continue to replicate and improve upon Snapchat's products
- The company must continuously innovate to maintain user engagement and advertiser appeal

## Privacy and Regulatory Headwinds
- Apple's iOS privacy updates have materially impacted ad targeting, measurement, and optimization capabilities
- Similar changes from Google, Firefox, Safari, and Chrome could further reduce advertising demand and pricing
- Regulatory scrutiny and potential mandated product changes pose ongoing risks

## User Engagement and Retention Risks
- Declining or stagnant daily active user (DAU) growth rates, with the company having 474 million DAUs as of December 31, 2025
- Low barriers to entry and switching costs that make it easy for users to migrate to competing platforms
- The majority of users being 18-34 years old, a demographic that may be less brand loyal and more trend-driven
- Difficulty penetrating other demographic segments
- Competition from other companies for user attention and engagement
- Various factors that could negatively affect retention, including poor product performance, security concerns, inadequate content, regulatory changes, and technical issues

## Advertising Revenue Concentration
- Heavy dependence on advertising revenue, which accounted for approximately 87% of total revenue in 2025 (down from 96% in 2023)
- Most advertisers lacking long-term commitments
- Risk of losing major advertising customers or experiencing reduced spending
- Advertiser concerns about the effectiveness and experimental nature of advertising solutions
- Economic and political instability affecting advertising budgets and forecasting

## Data Privacy and Regulatory Risks
- Increasing regulatory scrutiny on personal data collection, processing, and disclosure
- Restrictions on targeted advertising, particularly for teens
- Privacy controls implemented by Apple and potential similar changes by Google and other platforms that reduce targeting and measurement capabilities
- Compliance requirements with expanding data protection laws across multiple jurisdictions
- Potential significant monetary fines for regulatory violations
