[From Pinecone cache] # Primary Risk Factors Disclosed

The company discloses several significant risk factors related to its business operations:

## Medical Cost Management and Pricing Risks
The company assumes medical and administrative cost risks through its risk-based benefit products, which constitute nearly 80% of total consolidated revenues. Key risks include the inability to accurately estimate, price for, and manage medical costs. Small differences between predicted and actual medical costs can result in significant financial impacts. Various factors may cause actual costs to exceed estimates, including medical cost inflation, increased service utilization, provider billing intensity, unexpected population differences, new drug introductions or price increases, pandemics, climate change effects, and regulatory changes.

## Data Integrity and Information Systems Risks
The company depends heavily on the integrity and availability of data used to serve members, customers, and healthcare professionals. Risks include inaccurate or unreliable data, failures in maintaining data integrity, problems with technology products and artificial intelligence systems, and difficulties in consolidating, integrating, and upgrading information systems. The company also faces challenges in keeping pace with rapidly evolving information processing technology and regulatory standards.

## Cybersecurity and Data Security Risks
The company regularly processes large amounts of sensitive data, including protected personal information and proprietary information. It faces ongoing risks from cyberattacks, data breaches, ransomware, malware, and other security threats. The company previously experienced a cyberattack affecting its Change Healthcare business. Cybersecurity incidents could result in operational disruptions, loss of customers, financial losses, regulatory penalties, and reputational harm.

## Business Relationship Risks
The company's success depends on maintaining satisfactory relationships with healthcare payers, physicians, hospitals, and other service providers.
