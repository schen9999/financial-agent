- **Revenue Concerns**: The company faces slow, declining, or negative revenue growth rates

## Strategic Initiatives
- **Operational Optimization**: The company is executing cost-reduction initiatives, workforce reductions, and leadership changes
- **Portfolio Narrowing**: There are plans to exit or discontinue select product lines and operations in certain geographies
- **Margin Focus**: Efforts are underway to expand gross margins and improve cash generation

## External Pressures
- **Economic Uncertainty**: Inflation, high interest rates, and potential recessions pose significant risks
- **Trade Policy Risks**: Tariff uncertainties and trade wars could impact supply chains and product costs, particularly given the company's reliance on Canadian pea protein sourcing
- **Geopolitical Tensions**: Military conflicts and international instability create supply chain disruptions and cost volatility

## Operational Risks
- **Supply Chain Dependence**: Limited supplier relationships and reliance on co-manufacturers create vulnerability
- **Inventory Management**: Challenges in forecasting demand and managing excess inventory
- **Customer Concentration**: Limited distributor base and potential loss of significant customers

## Financial Position
- **Capital Needs**: Significant indebtedness and potential need for additional financing
- **Balance Sheet Constraints**: Covenant restrictions on debt limit strategic flexibility
