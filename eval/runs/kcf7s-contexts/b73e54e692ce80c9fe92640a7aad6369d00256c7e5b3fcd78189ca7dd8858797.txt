[From Pinecone cache] # Key Takeaways from Cal-Maine Foods' Recent SEC Filings

## Company Overview
Cal-Maine Foods is the largest egg company in the United States and a leading player in the egg-based food industry. Founded in 1957 and headquartered in Ridgeland, Mississippi, the company operates a comprehensive portfolio spanning conventional to specialty eggs (cage-free, organic, pasture-raised, etc.) and prepared foods products.

## Strategic Growth Initiatives
The company is pursuing several key growth strategies:
- Increasing the proportion of specialty shell eggs in its sales mix
- Expanding prepared foods and egg products businesses
- Strengthening its portfolio of branded offerings (including Eggland's Best®, Land O'Lakes®, Van's®, and Crepini®)
- Pursuing strategic acquisitions and organic investments

## Recent Acquisitions
Cal-Maine has been actively acquiring complementary businesses:
- **Echo Lake Foods** (June 2025, ~$289.5M) - Prepared foods producer
- **Creighton Brothers** (March 2026, ~$129.3M) - Shell egg and egg products operations
- **Van's Foods** (May 2026, ~$24.8M) - Frozen bakery products
- **Clean Egg** (October 2025, ~$23.7M) - Cage-free and free-range layers
- **ISE America** (Q1 FY2025, ~$4.7M layers) - Northeast and Mid-Atlantic operations
- **Crepini Foods** (Q2 FY2025, 51% stake, ~$6.75M) - Egg wraps and specialty products

## Capacity Expansion Projects
Significant production capacity expansions are underway through mid-to-late fiscal 2027, expected to grow prepared foods capacity by over 30 percent.

## Risk Factors
Key risks include HPAI outbreaks, feed cost volatility, market price fluctuations, integration challenges from acquisitions, and competitive pressures.

[From Pinecone cache] # Primary Risk Factors Disclosed

The company discloses several significant risk factors affecting its operations:

**Market and Pricing Risks:**
- Fluctuations in wholesale shell egg market prices
- Changes in demand for shell eggs and prepared foods offerings
- Government, customer, and consumer reactions to high egg market prices, including potential new regulations

**Operational and Cost Risks:**
- Increases in feed costs for shell egg operations and input costs for prepared foods
- Risks and hazards inherent in shell egg, egg products, and prepared foods operations, including disease, pests, weather conditions, and product recall potential
- The current outbreak of Highly Pathogenic Avian Influenza (HPAI) affecting poultry in the U.S., Canada, and other countries, which impacted operations in fiscal 2024 and again in March 2026

**Business Development Risks:**
- Ability to predict and meet demand for cage-free and other specialty eggs
- Ability to successfully integrate and manage recently acquired businesses and realize expected benefits such as synergies, cost savings, and margin expansion
- Risks that conditions for completing pending acquisitions may not be met

**Competitive and Strategic Risks:**
- Ability to compete effectively with existing competitors and new market entrants
- Ability to retain existing customers and acquire new customers
- Ability to produce, supply, and distribute products efficiently and reliably

**Financial and External Risks:**
- Potential changes in inflation, interest rates, and trade and tariff policies
- Loss or expiration of registered trademarks or intellectual property
- Adverse results in pending litigation
- Global instability from geopolitical conflicts and other uncertainties
